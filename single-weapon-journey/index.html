Throw. Stick. Climb.

Real physics, one gesture.

  • Real physics

    The blade bites into wood and holds. The handle bounces off stone. Glass shatters and ropes give way.

  • One gesture

    Drag to throw the axe, then drag again to turn it in mid-air. Play one-handed with the thumb pad, or touch the axe directly.

  • 14 unique levels

    Smash glass, cut ropes, ride spinning wheels and moving planks, outrun crumbling wood and catch the updrafts.

  • Collect every shard

    Three crystals hide in every level. Shatter them all, then go back for a faster run.

  • Race the world

    Every level has a leaderboard. Pick your runner name, race the ghost of your best run and watch your route replay.

  • Calm and neon

    Ancient stone ruins, neon light and a calm, lonely soundtrack. No ads, no account to create.
